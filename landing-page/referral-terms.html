Referral terms

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Your code

  • Every annual subscriber has a personal referral code and link, shown in My CPD under Refer a colleague.

For the colleague you refer

  • A new annual subscriber who uses your code gets £10 off their first year. Later years are at the normal price.
  • The code works only on a new annual subscription. It does not work on a single course, a Field Work series or the free course, and it cannot be used with another code.
  • It does not work for someone who has subscribed before.

For you

  • When your colleague’s payment goes through, you get £10 off your next renewal.
  • You get one £10 credit for each colleague who subscribes with your code. Credits add up to a free year, the price of one renewal.
  • You cannot use your own code.
  • If your colleague’s subscription is refunded, the credit for it is removed.
  • Credit comes off your renewal. It has no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Changes

  • We may change or end the scheme. Credit already on your account stays there.
